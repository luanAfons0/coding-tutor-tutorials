# Who am I

Hi — I'm Luan, a developer learning **how computers really work**, one idea at a time.

I write **TypeScript** every day (with the occasional reluctant detour into C#). That means I've spent most of my time in comfortable, garbage-collected, high-level land — where memory "just happens." Lately I've been deliberately walking into the uncomfortable side: **C**, manual memory, pointers, and the bytes underneath everything.

I'm based in **Brazil** 🇧🇷, study *Análise e Desenvolvimento de Sistemas* at night, and work full-time during the day — so this is about an hour a day, carved out because I actually want to understand the machine, not just use it.

My goal isn't a certificate or a job title. It's simple: **become a better programmer by really learning how things work.**

**What I'm on right now:** building data structures and small systems from scratch in C — pointers and the heap, dynamic arrays and linked lists, and (further ahead) a Redis-style store, a tiny SQLite-style database, and a Game Boy emulator.

Welcome to my journey from *"I use computers"* to *"I understand computers."* 🚀
